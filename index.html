How to read the graph

An arrow points from a parent term to a term identified while studying it. Dashed arrows connect related passages or word forms across the study. Colors identify workbook tabs. Relative node sizes are compressed so small terms remain selectable. Total matches come from row 2. Highlighted counts come from the colored verse cells and exclude gray. These are counts per term, so a verse may count for several terms. While a term is selected, spaced orange pulses travel in from parent terms and spaced blue pulses travel out along its directed connections. Motion follows your device preference by default.
